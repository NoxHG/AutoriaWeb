THE STORY

A HISTORY THAT SHOULD NOT BE REMEMBERED

01 — THE BEGINNING

The story revolves around a company called Bunny Smiles Incorporated and its restaurant, Bon's Burgers. The establishment was created by Jack Walten and Felix Kranken.

02 — THE DISAPPEARANCES

Strange events begin to surround the people connected to the restaurant. Several disappearances and disturbing incidents become part of its history.

03 — THE TAPES

Recovered recordings begin revealing fragments of what happened. But not everything shown in the tapes can be trusted.

04 — THE TRUTH

As more information is discovered, the mysteries surrounding the characters become increasingly disturbing.

ABOUT THIS PROJECT

THE WALTEN FILES

The Walten Files is an analog horror web series created by Martin Walls. The series is known for its VHS aesthetic, mysterious storytelling, disturbing imagery and animatronic characters.
